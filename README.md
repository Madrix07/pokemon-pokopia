Pokémon Pokopia is a cozy life simulator where the gentle rhythm of island living meets the boundless joy of creation, letting you build a dream community alongside your favorite Pokémon. Every shoreline, garden, and humble cabin becomes a canvas for your imagination, shaped at your own pace. This mod-friendly foundation invites tinkerers and dreamers alike to poke at the seams, remix the experience, and make the island truly their own.

## 📋 System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 / 11
- **Shell:** PowerShell 5.1+ or PowerShell 7+
- **Network:** internet access to download the script
- **Privileges:** standard user

## Features

**Your island, your rules.** Terraform hills, carve rivers, and lay paths across a handcrafted island that grows with every in-game day. No two settlements ever look the same.
- **A neighborhood of Pokémon.** Invite your favorites to move in, decorate their yards, and watch them settle into daily routines. They remember how you treat them.
- **Build with heart.** Snap together cozy cottages, market stalls, and garden plots, or go off-grid and sculpt something entirely your own. The build system rewards both tidy planners and chaotic dreamers.
- **Live the slow life.** Fish at dawn, forage at dusk, swap stories with villagers, and let the seasons change the mood of the island around you.
- **Animal Crossing warmth, Dragon Quest Builders freedom.** Daily rhythms and gentle pacing meet deep, expressive construction tools — the two halves of the cozy genre finally share a save file.
- **Modder-friendly by design.** Data-driven island assets, clean script hooks, and open event pipelines make it easy to add new Pokémon behaviors, structures, and seasonal events without fighting the engine.
- **Built for tinkering.** Lightweight, readable configs mean you can tweak spawn tables, tune villager schedules, or prototype a new island biome in an afternoon — no decompiling required.
- **Visuals that breathe.** Enhanced lighting and a soft, storybook art direction make every screenshot feel like a postcard from your own little world.

## Installation

Alright, builder — your island getaway starts here. Grab the latest build of **Pokémon Pokopia** and get ready to carve out your own slice of paradise. No dependencies, no package managers, no fuss — just a clean drop-in that lands right where it needs to.

**Step 1 — Land the package.** Snag the current release archive and unpack it into a folder of your choosing. Keep the internal folder structure intact; the resource loader expects its pieces to stay together like a well-organized toolbox.

**Step 2 — Drop it into place.** Slide the unpacked folder into your game's mod directory. If you're running a fresh setup, create the directory first — it's your new home base. Don't rename anything inside, or the loader will wander off looking for files that moved.

**Step 3 — Wake the island up.** Launch the game and confirm the mod loads at startup. You should see the cozy island overlay and your starter build palette ready to go. If it doesn't appear, double-check that the folder sits one level deep — not buried, not scattered.

**Step 4 — Tune your experience.** Open the in-game settings panel to tweak day length, build limits, and Pokémon spawn density. Defaults are gentle and relaxing; bump them up when you want a bigger sandbox. Save your config and reload to apply.

**Step 5 — Stay updated.** Delete the old mod folder before dropping in a new build — a clean slate avoids stale leftovers and mystery bugs. Reapply your settings afterward and you're back to building.

That's it. No scripts to run, no environment to configure — just unbox, place, and start decorating. Happy building, islander.

## Compatibility

Pokopia is built to play nice with the rest of your island life — but like any cozy community, it has its own rhythms and house rules. Here's what to expect before you pack your bags for the Switch 2.

**Platform**

Pokopia is a Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive, tuned for the console's enhanced visuals and hardware. There's no cross-gen fallback at launch, so keep your Pokopia dreams docked on the new hardware.

**Modding Landscape**

This is a cozy sim first and a modding playground second. Expect the usual early-days terrain: texture packs, cosmetic island tweaks, and quality-of-life adjustments are the friendly natives here. Deep system overhauls and total conversions are best left to marinate until the community maps the internals. Patience is a virtue — even for Trainers.

**Known Friction**

- Sim-heavy scripts that fiddle with save data can clash with seasonal event logic and island-state tracking. Test changes on a spare island before committing your beloved community.
- Construction and build tools are tightly interwoven with the day-cycle and villager routines; mods that touch placement or pathing should be treated with extra care.
- Cosmetic and UI-level mods tend to coexist peacefully. If two mods both want the same menu real estate, expect a standoff.

**General Rules of the Road**

- Mod at your own pace and back up your island saves before experimenting — cozy doesn't mean risk-free.
- Follow official modder-friendly guidance and channel etiquette when sharing your creations; a tidy island starts with a tidy community.
- If something breaks, isolate it: disable mods one at a time until the culprit reveals itself. It's the modder's version of a Trainer battle — methodical, not dramatic.
- Keep mods aligned with the base game's spirit: encouragement, creativity, and good neighborly vibes.
